Privacy Policy

How Anova Viral handles data — clearly and simply.

Last updated · July 14, 2026

01Who we are

Anova Viral ("we", "our") is a content production studio tool that helps creators produce and publish video content to their own social media accounts — YouTube, TikTok, Instagram and Facebook. Contact: anovaviralsystem@gmail.com.

02What data we access

Anova Viral connects only to accounts owned and authorized by the operator of the tool, through the official APIs of each platform (Google/YouTube, Meta/Instagram/Facebook, TikTok):

  • Basic profile information of the connected accounts (name, username, avatar).
  • Publishing permissions to upload video content to those accounts.
  • Public performance metrics of the published content (views, likes, comments).

03What we do not do

  • We do not collect, sell, rent or share personal data with third parties.
  • We do not access data from other users of the platforms.
  • We do not use advertising trackers or analytics cookies on this site.
  • We do not store passwords — authentication runs through each platform's official OAuth flow.

04Data storage

Access tokens granted through OAuth are stored locally on the operator's own equipment and are used exclusively to publish content and read metrics of the connected accounts. They can be revoked at any time from the security settings of each platform.

05Data deletion

To request deletion of any data related to your accounts, or to revoke access, write to anovaviralsystem@gmail.com — or remove the app's access directly from your Google, Meta or TikTok security settings. Locally stored tokens are deleted upon disconnection.

06Third-party services

Content publishing is carried out through the official APIs of YouTube (Google LLC), Instagram and Facebook (Meta Platforms, Inc.) and TikTok (TikTok Pte. Ltd.). Use of those platforms is governed by their own privacy policies.

07Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the latest revision.

Questions?

We answer privacy inquiries at anovaviralsystem@gmail.com — usually within 48 hours.
